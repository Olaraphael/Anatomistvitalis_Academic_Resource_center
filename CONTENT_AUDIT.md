# Anatomistvitalis Academic Resource Center — Content Audit

Audit tracker created: 13 October 2026.

## Scope and method

The `topics/` directory contains **117 topic articles**. An initial structural scan inventoried all 117 article directories and checked for common learning-page features: objectives, assessment/answer blocks, image/figure elements, and further-reading/source markers. This is a triage scan, not a claim that every sentence and every external image has already passed full expert review.

## Audit standards

- **Accuracy:** correct anatomy/physiology, current terminology, no unsupported causal or clinical claims.
- **Depth:** explain structure → function → mechanism → clinical/practical relevance, not headings alone.
- **Learning design:** clear objectives, prerequisite links, useful definitions, worked examples, recall questions with explanatory answers.
- **Visual quality:** real specimen, radiology or histology images where appropriate; original diagrams for pathways and mechanisms; meaningful alt text and captions.
- **Sources:** reputable textbooks, open textbooks, guidelines or primary literature; external images must have a checked licence and attribution.
- **Clinical safety:** distinguish education from diagnosis or treatment and flag emergencies appropriately.
- **Technical quality:** valid links, correct relative image paths, mobile-friendly layout and cache refresh when necessary.

## Important limitations and next pass

1. A structural feature check cannot guarantee scientific accuracy; each article still needs a detailed sentence-by-sentence content review.
2. External image URLs and their licence records must be checked individually, and images should be verified in the deployed page rather than inferred from source markup alone.
3. The next pass should work systematically by curriculum stage, starting with foundations and histology, then regional gross anatomy, embryology, neuroanatomy, physiology, pathology, and rehabilitation.
4. Any disputed or advanced medical claim should be checked against a current standard reference or guideline before being presented as fact.

Do not treat this tracker as a certificate that the full audit is complete. Statuses are intentionally explicit about what has and has not been reviewed.
