Structure spotlight

Human heart — anterior view

The heart is a muscular pump in the middle mediastinum. Its right side sends blood to the lungs; its left side drives blood through the systemic circulation.

  • 1Aorta — carries oxygenated blood from the left ventricle to the systemic circulation.
  • 2Pulmonary trunk — carries deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle towards the lungs.
  • 3Right atrium — receives systemic venous blood.
  • 4Left atrium — receives oxygenated blood from the pulmonary veins.
  • 5Right ventricle — pumps blood into the pulmonary circulation.
  • 6Left ventricle — generates pressure for systemic circulation.
Clinical connection

The left ventricular wall is thicker than the right because systemic circulation requires higher pressure than pulmonary circulation.
