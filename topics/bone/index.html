Bone tissue and remodelling

Bone is living mineralised connective tissue. Its extracellular matrix contains type I collagen and mineral, mainly hydroxyapatite. Compact and trabecular bone differ in organisation, but both undergo continuous remodelling.

High-yield study points

  • Osteoblasts form bone matrix and support mineralisation.
  • Osteoclasts resorb bone.
  • Osteocytes are mature cells embedded in matrix and help sense mechanical loading.
  • Remodelling supports repair, mineral homeostasis and adaptation to loading.
  • Bone growth, modelling and remodelling are related but distinct processes.

Clinical connection

Bone remodelling responds to mechanical forces, hormones, age, nutrition and disease. Bone is living tissue, not an inert scaffold.
