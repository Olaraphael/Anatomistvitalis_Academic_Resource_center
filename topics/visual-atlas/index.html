Image selection and labelling rules

  • Real photographs: dissected specimens, bones, organs and clinical/radiological images when the goal is realistic recognition.
  • Labelled diagrams: pathways, foramina, nerve branches, embryological stages, spatial relationships and structures difficult to distinguish in photographs.
  • Histology: actual micrographs with tissue identity, stain and magnification/scale if provided by the source.
  • Attribution: source title, author or institution, source-page link, exact licence, and any modifications.
  • Commercial caution: OpenStax's Anatomy and Physiology 2e is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so its content is non-commercial unless separate permission is obtained. Wikimedia files have individual licences; do not treat the whole site as having one licence. OpenStax licence details.
