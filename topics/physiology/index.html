Recommended learning order

Physiology explains how the structures studied in anatomy work. Begin with cells, membranes, transport and homeostasis; then progress through excitable tissues, blood, cardiovascular, respiratory, renal, gastrointestinal, endocrine, reproductive and special-sense physiology.

Physiology learning standard

Each system should explain the normal mechanism first, its regulation second, the way variables are measured third, and disturbances or clinical applications last. Use pathway diagrams, labelled graphs, worked calculations and case questions. Every quantitative equation must define its variables and units.

Anatomy and physiology are closely linked, but they should not be mixed into one confusing list. Each subject has its own hub and learning sequence, with cross-links where structure explains function.

Study standard: use the article explanations alongside labelled visuals, self-test questions and cited further reading.
