Required research workflow

  1. Define a focused, answerable question and justify its significance.
  2. Search credible databases and keep a reproducible search record.
  3. Choose a design appropriate to the question; specify population, sample size rationale, variables and outcomes.
  4. Obtain appropriate ethics approval before collecting data, where required.
  5. Use validated methods, document procedures and preserve original data.
  6. Choose analyses that match the design and data; report estimates, uncertainty and limitations.
  7. Write with real, verified references and the appropriate reporting guideline.
  8. Choose a legitimate journal or repository and check fees and licence terms before submission.

Never invent references, ethics approval numbers, data values or study outcomes. When a detail is unknown, state the limitation and seek confirmation from the responsible researcher or institution.

Study standard: use the article explanations alongside labelled visuals, self-test questions and cited further reading.
