Clinical learning framework

Organise each condition around presentation, relevant anatomy and physiology, assessment, functional impact, measurable goals, intervention rationale, precautions, reassessment and referral criteria. Distinguish educational content from individual treatment advice.

Clinical workflow

  1. Clarify the person's goals and obtain consent.
  2. Screen for red flags and determine whether referral or medical review is needed.
  3. Assess impairments, activity limitations, participation and contextual factors.
  4. Choose meaningful outcome measures and record a baseline.
  5. Agree goals and select interventions with a clear rationale and appropriate precautions.
  6. Monitor response, reassess outcomes and adapt the plan collaboratively.
