Meninges and cerebrospinal fluid

The brain and spinal cord are covered by dura mater, arachnoid mater and pia mater. Cerebrospinal fluid cushions the central nervous system, supports chemical stability and circulates through ventricles and subarachnoid spaces.

Learning objectives

  • Name the dura mater, arachnoid mater and pia mater in superficial-to-deep order.
  • Distinguish the epidural, subdural and subarachnoid spaces, noting which are potential spaces.
  • Relate meningeal anatomy to cerebrospinal fluid circulation and common haemorrhage patterns.

High-yield study points

  • Dura is the tough outer meninx; arachnoid is the middle membrane; pia closely follows the CNS surface.
  • The subarachnoid space between arachnoid and pia contains CSF and major vessels.
  • Most CSF is produced by choroid plexuses within the ventricles.
  • CSF flows through the ventricular system into subarachnoid spaces and is returned to venous circulation through several drainage pathways.
  • Dural folds support cranial contents and enclose venous sinuses.

Clinical connection

Meningitis is inflammation of the meninges. Fever with severe headache, neck stiffness, altered consciousness or neurological deficit needs urgent medical evaluation.

Memory aid / mnemonic

Dura → Arachnoid → Pia (“DAP”). CSF is in the subarachnoid space, between arachnoid and pia.

Revision tip: recall the cue without looking, then explain the anatomical reason behind it.

Check your understanding

  1. List the meninges from superficial to deep.
    Reveal answer

    Dura mater, arachnoid mater, pia mater.

  2. Where does cerebrospinal fluid circulate around the brain and spinal cord?
    Reveal answer

    In the subarachnoid space between arachnoid mater and pia mater.

  3. Which vessel injury is classically associated with an epidural haematoma in the temporal region?
    Reveal answer

    Middle meningeal artery injury is a classic association, often following a temporal bone fracture; real cases vary.

Check your understanding: meninges and spaces

  1. Name the three cranial meninges from superficial to deep.
    Answer and explanation

    Dura mater, arachnoid mater and pia mater. The pia closely invests the brain surface and follows sulci; the arachnoid bridges over sulci.

  2. Where is cerebrospinal fluid located around the brain?
    Answer and explanation

    In the subarachnoid space between arachnoid and pia. The “subdural space” is normally a potential space that can become apparent in certain pathological conditions.

  3. Why is the epidural space described differently in the cranium and vertebral canal?
    Answer and explanation

    In the vertebral canal, the epidural space is a real space containing fat and venous plexuses. In the cranium, dura is normally closely applied to the inner skull, and an epidural space usually develops pathologically when dura separates from bone.
