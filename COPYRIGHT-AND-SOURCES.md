## Rules for adding future images

Before adding any third-party image, verify that the licence for that specific file permits the intended use. Record the image title, creator, original source page, direct file URL where available, exact licence and licence URL, any adaptations made, and the attribution text displayed with the image.

Do not assume an image is free to reuse just because it appears in Google Images, social media, a textbook preview, or a public website. Some licences prohibit commercial use or adaptations. Avoid assets with unclear ownership or terms. Keep evidence of licence terms with project records.
